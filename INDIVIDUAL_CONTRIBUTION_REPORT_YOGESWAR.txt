    a cashier bills an order or an employee validates a retailer, all open browser 
    tabs immediately refetch live data without page reloads.

        const total = document.documentElement.scrollHeight - window.innerHeight;
        setScrollProgress((window.scrollY / total) * 100);
      };
      window.addEventListener('scroll', handleScroll, { passive: true });
      return () => window.removeEventListener('scroll', handleScroll);
    }, []);

[5M] 6. INDIVIDUAL CODE EXPLANATION & VIVA DEFENSE GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Be prepared to answer these core questions during viva evaluation:

Q1: "Explain how your Revenue Model works in code."
A1: "The platform generates revenue through two distinct streams implemented in 
    RevenuePage_admin.jsx and PlatformRevenueService:
    1. SaaS Tier Subscriptions: Retailers subscribe to Free (₹0), Vyapar Pro 
       (₹799/mo), or Enterprise Hub (₹3,499/mo). We aggregate these into Monthly 
       Recurring Revenue (MRR).
    2. Platform POS Commissions: Every physical transaction processed through 
       the cashier POS incurs a 2.0% platform fee. In RevenuePage_admin, we fetch 
       transactions via fetchPlatformCommissions() and compute gross revenue vs 
       net commission retained by the platform."

Q2: "Walk me through the verification lifecycle of a new Retailer."
A2: "1. A store owner registers via RegisterPage.jsx. Their profile is saved with 
       status 'pending_verification'.
     2. When they attempt to sign in, App.jsx intercepts their session and renders 
        VerificationPendingPage.jsx, barring them from accessing inventory.
     3. The retailer's dossier appears in ValidationsPage_employee.jsx.
     4. An employee clicks 'Review' and inspects the trade license/GSTIN.
     5. The employee opens VerificationActionModal.jsx to approve or reject with 
        audit remarks.
     6. A PATCH request is dispatched to /api/employees/assignments/:id/resolve.
     7. Upon approval, status transitions to 'Active', and DataSyncContext broadcasts 
        an update unlocking the full Retailer Dashboard."

Q3: "How does StoreDashboardPage_admin isolate data for a specific store?"
A3: "It accepts a storeId prop (defaulting to the active store). In loadScopedData, 
     we execute Promise.allSettled fetching all stores, products, transactions, and 
     users. We then filter products and transactions strictly by 
     item.storeId === selectedStoreId. This guarantees that stock levels, sales, 
     and cashier receipts shown belong exclusively to that physical branch."

Q4: "Why use DataSyncContext instead of Redux or raw localStorage?"
A4: "Redux introduces excessive boilerplate for this scale. DataSyncContext leverages 
     React's native Context API combined with the browser's window storage event. 
     When an entity changes, broadcastSync() increments a sync counter. Subscribed 
     views observe this change via useEffect and trigger background data refetches, 
     giving us real-time multi-tab synchronization with minimal overhead."
